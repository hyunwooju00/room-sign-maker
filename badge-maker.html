PRINT CHECKLIST

Check one sheet before the full run.

  1. Choose the same paper size in this tool and in your printer dialog.
  2. Use portrait and 100% scale. Choose no margins when available, and turn browser headers and footers off.
  3. Print one sheet on plain paper. Measure a badge: it should be 3 × 4 inches (76.2 × 101.6 mm). Cut along the dotted borders and check your holder before printing all sheets.

These are cut-out inserts on plain paper, not a preset for perforated label stock. Badge holder sizes vary. Role text is for display only, not access control.
